# Known Limits

This file documents the known limitations, boundaries, and constraints of this repository's content.

## What This Repo Does Not Prove

- This repo does not prove that the operating system is free of defects.
- This repo does not prove that all edge cases are covered.
- This repo does not prove that external systems behave as expected.

## What May Be Wrong

- Any generated content may contain overclaims, contradictions, or unsupported assertions.
- Any case study may rely on incomplete or outdated public sources.
- Any formal model may contain assumptions that do not hold in all environments.

## What Is Missing

- Items marked as TODO or DRAFT are incomplete.
- Items without test evidence are unproved assertions.
- Items without source references are unsourced.

## How to Challenge

- Open an issue with the specific claim you are challenging.
- Provide evidence or reasoning for why the claim is incorrect, incomplete, or misleading.
- Reference the sealed law principle that applies.

All challenges are reviewed. No challenge is rejected without a documented reason.
